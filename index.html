💡 Local Intel

🔌 Power: Ireland uses Type G plugs (UK-style 3-prong), 230V. Bring an adapter — US plugs won't fit.
💶 Currency: Euro (€). Cards accepted everywhere. Minimal cash needed — maybe €20 for small vendors.
🕐 Timezone: IST (Irish Standard Time) = UTC+1. Same as UK. 6 hours ahead of CDT.
🗣️ Language: English + Irish Gaelic on signs. Everyone speaks English.
📱 Apps: Download Leap Top-Up, Free Now (Irish Uber), and TFI Journey Planner.
🛡️ Safety: State Dept Level 1 — Exercise normal precautions. Watch for pickpockets in Temple Bar and Grafton St. Dial 999 or 112 for emergencies.
🧥 Pack: Rain jacket, layers, waterproof shoes. Dublin drizzle is real. Highs in the low 60s°F — leave the shorts.
🚨 Jun 22 Protest: National rally at Garden of Remembrance, Parnell Sq, 2 PM IST. O'Connell St closures, bus/LUAS diversions. Leave for airport by 10 AM. Garda presence expected.
